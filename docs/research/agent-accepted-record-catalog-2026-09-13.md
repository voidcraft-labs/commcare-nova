# Accepted record preparation

The construction-only garden trial on `9cb2b4a1` stopped at the production
attempt budget after 22 Luna requests, before creating an app. It cost
$0.27702225. The reviewed harness allowed 60 construction requests; that cap
did not stop this run. Total recorded generation spend is $31.54603425 across
311 completed requests, with no pending reservations at this checkpoint.

The trial replayed the fifth garden trial's exact accepted design and clean
review, with explicit fixture provenance. It is evidence about construction
from that design, not a fresh design success. Private artifacts are under
`/private/tmp/nova-agent-research-20260912/build-short-field-1`, with a separate
source manifest. No prompts, user artifacts, or request bodies are published here.

The builder repeatedly submitted record definitions that were already fixed by
the accepted design. It also tried to declare a later workflow's record while
working on registration. The refusal incorrectly emphasized case-type spelling
instead of scope. Reads and repeated case-list configuration consumed the rest
of the attempt. The run never reached the weekly form's grouped field references,
so it provides no live quality comparison for that change.

Nova can determine catalog structure without asking the builder to restate it.
Preparation now derives exact case/property keys, parents, intrinsic types, and
inline choices from the accepted design. It runs through the shared catalog tool
and private workspace before the model starts. A stable request ID and budget
claim cover process replacement; successful no-op receipts cover later workflows
that use existing definitions. Earlier wording and rule refinements survive.
The executor retains catalog inspection and correction, while declaration and
renaming remain available to the editor and MCP.

Review exposed three semantic traps in an initial implementation: treating
business names such as `status` as runtime metadata, interpreting braces in
accepted labels as answer references, and retaining executor rename authority
after making property keys server-owned. The final boundary keeps business
properties distinct, stores accepted wording as literal canonical text, and
keeps accepted keys fixed during construction. The explicit `case_name` and
`external_id` design conventions retain their scalar meaning and require unique,
text-typed declarations. Contextual form rules are not copied into the catalog.

Separately, real local submission checks exercised the earlier partial garden
app saved by the 30-request trial. They proved registration requirements and
numeric bounds in FormEngine, then used authorized Postgres submissions to
create two plots named “Rose plot” with distinct identities and correct only
the selected plot. Independent review confirmed the source blueprint matched
the tested snapshot and that the observations were scoped accurately. These
checks do not establish weekly history, navigation, browser orchestration,
native device behavior, or a complete garden app.

This slice removes redundant construction decisions. It does not establish
semantic completion or replace the remaining input-binding, app-quality, and
reviewed-edit work.
