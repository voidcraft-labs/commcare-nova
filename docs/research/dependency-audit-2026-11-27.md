# Dependency audit, 2026-11-27

This change carries [Dependabot #959](https://github.com/voidcraft-labs/commcare-nova/pull/959)
at `025175cb1fa9680bf29b54f96f49b00c9128a264` onto main
`767c8919ef252a64c54fca1ec2caaeab3f9ebb7e`. The package manifest and lockfile
match the bot's head byte for byte. The proposal includes 42 updates, three of
which only change lockfile resolutions. The React type override moves with its
direct pin. The Workflow override, profiler pins, install-script policy and
Node 24.21.0/npm 12.0.2 pins are retained. Upstream proof pins are tracked
independently in [#734](https://github.com/voidcraft-labs/commcare-nova/pull/734).

the default five-second budget on the replacement. It now has a 15-second

## Residual advisories

Read-only `npm audit` reported 20 affected dependency entries (17 moderate, 3
high). The high entries are the nested Undici under `@ai-sdk/workflow`, nested
Zod under `agent-react-devtools`, and that profiler parent. Root Undici and Zod
were outside these audit entries. The moderate entries include the Sentry and
Google SQL chains, glob/minimatch through Testcontainers and archive helpers,
and the shadcn/ts-morph chain. The pinned profiler, Workflow line and unrelated
transitive chains remain separate upgrade decisions. npm's suggested
Testcontainers remediation is a major downgrade and its profiler remediation is
a change to the separately audited profiler pin.

The PR's verification covers the built image, migrated Postgres, real browser
smoke and static proof lane. The scheduled reader lane and paid model probes
are outside this verification.
