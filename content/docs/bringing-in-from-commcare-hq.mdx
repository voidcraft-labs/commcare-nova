---
title: Bringing in an app from CommCare HQ
description: How to carry on in Nova with an app you already have in CommCare HQ, what Nova checks first, and what happens when you publish back.
---

If your app already lives in CommCare HQ, you can bring it in to Nova and keep
working on it here. Nova reads the app with your CommCare HQ account, shows you
what will come in, and creates nothing until you say so. Nothing changes in
CommCare HQ until you publish from Nova.

## Before you start

You need a CommCare HQ API key saved in Settings, and a project space whose plan
includes API access. Your account there needs the Edit Apps and Access APIs
permissions.

Nova needs API access because it is how Nova learns each form's own ID, which is
what lets things in CommCare HQ that open a form, such as an SMS keyword, keep
working after you publish back.

## Choosing the app

There are three ways in: **Bring in from CommCare HQ** beside **New app** on
your apps page, **You can bring it in** on an apps page with no apps yet, and
**Bring in an app from CommCare HQ** under the chat box when you start a new app.

Choose the Nova Project to add it to, the project space, and the app. An app
that reads lookup tables stays in the Project it's added to, so it's worth
choosing the Project your team shares.

One CommCare HQ app is managed by one Nova app. If someone already brought an
app in, the list says so: you can open it, or it tells you who to ask.

## While Nova reads

Reading most apps takes a few seconds. An app with lots of pictures and sounds
takes longer, and Nova shows how many it has checked. You can leave the page:
nothing is created until you choose **Bring it in**, and Nova reads the app
afresh when you come back.

The page's address names the app, so you can keep it or send it to a teammate.

## When Nova can bring it in

Nova shows you one review before anything is created.

**Your app** has the name, which you can change, and what comes in: its menus,
forms, questions, languages and case types.

**Where workers use this app**: Nova proposes where the app runs from what it
uses and from its Web App setting in CommCare HQ. You can choose differently
here or later in App settings.

**Lookup tables** and **Places**: the tables and places the app reads come in as
copies that CommCare HQ keeps managing. [Project data](/docs/project-data) and
[Organization](/docs/organization) say what that means.

If your Project already has a lookup table by the same name, Nova asks which one
this app should use and says what each answer does to your other apps.

**Stays as it is in CommCare HQ** lists the settings Nova leaves alone when it
publishes. [Publishing](/docs/publishing) shows the same list once the app is
in.

Nova brings in the expressions of your app: display conditions, validation,
calculations, case list filters and search filters. It keeps the questions, case
properties and tables they name as links, so renaming one later updates every
expression that reads it.

Nothing is created until you choose **Bring it in**.

## When Nova can't bring it in yet

Nova brings in an app only when it can hold every part of it exactly, so nothing
is lost when you publish back. When it can't, it creates nothing and lists every
reason it found, grouped by where it is in your app.

Each reason has one of three labels.

- **Nova can't hold this yet.** There is nothing for you to change. You can
  check again whenever you like.
- **Nova doesn't bring this in.** The app can come in once it no longer uses
  this. If your team relies on it, the app is best kept in CommCare HQ.
- **Something to change in CommCare HQ.** Once this is changed in CommCare HQ,
  you can check again and Nova will read the app afresh.

Under the list Nova says: When you check again, Nova may find more to name.

Nova can't bring in an app whose case list column, filter, display condition or
search setting calls a function such as `int()` or `substr()`. It lists each one
with where it sits in your app. Inside a form, Nova can bring in calculations
that use `int()` or `substr()`.

Two more things are listed with where they sit, since Nova would save or find
something different from what CommCare HQ does today:

- A date and time question, or a hidden value that works out a date and time,
  saved straight to a case property. CommCare HQ keeps only its day there, and
  Nova keeps the whole moment.
- A search filter Nova would keep as part of the case list's filter, in a menu
  whose forms read its search, where the phone would read it differently from
  CommCare HQ's search. A filter that compares a case property with `!=` or
  with `not()` around `=`, compares with text that starts or ends with a
  space, or reads the worker's user data is one of these: CommCare HQ reads
  the spaces at the ends of a saved value, and the phone reads the value
  without them.

Nova keeps no copy of the list. **Copy the list** lets you take it with you, and
**Check again** reads the app afresh whenever you like.

Nova doesn't bring in part of an app.

Nova reads only so much of an app's lookup tables: a table holding more data
than Nova reads for one table is listed under **Nova can't hold this yet**, and
an app whose tables together hold more lookup data than Nova brings in is too.
That limit is Nova's, not the app's.

Nova also gives each app only so much checking for its size. An app that takes
more checking than that is listed under **Nova can't hold this yet** as an app
that takes more checking than Nova gives one app. That limit is Nova's too, and
it counts the checking itself rather than time, so checking again gives the same
answer. Looking for changes made in CommCare HQ works within the same limit.

When Nova is reading several apps from CommCare HQ at once, it may ask you to
try again in a minute or two. If this happens during your read, you can choose
**Check again** to have Nova read the app afresh.

## After it's in

The app opens in the builder, and **App setup → Publishing** already shows the
project space it came from.

You can keep working on it in Nova and publish back to the same app in its
project space.

When the app changes in CommCare HQ after you bring it in, your next publish
stops and lists what changed. You can bring those changes in to this app,
publish over them, stop publishing to that app, or close the dialog and decide
later. Nova also checks when you open the app, and offers **See what changed in
CommCare HQ** in the builder's header if something did.
[Changes made in CommCare HQ](./publishing.mdx#changes-made-in-commcare-hq) says
what each one does.

Publishing to another project space makes a new app there. A copy your team
already keeps there stays as it is.

## What the first publish changes

Nova writes an app in its own way, so the first time you publish an app you
brought in, some things will differ in CommCare HQ. Nova lists them before you
bring the app in, and again in the Publish dialog before that first publish:
what workers will notice, and what changes only in how CommCare HQ stores the
app.

The list names each change once, in plain words. Among them: each form's title
takes the form's name, Nova writes each form's case settings and each case
list's settings in its own way, and a choice with no name leaves a case list's
Sort by on Android.

A wording saved on CommCare HQ's app translations page, such as a menu's name
in one language, the app's name workers see on phones and in Web Apps, or a
case list column's header, comes in as Nova's wording for that text, and
publishing keeps it as it is. The text it stands in for keeps its own words.
A saved wording that changes nothing, because it is blank, names nothing in
the app, or matches what CommCare HQ writes there anyway, is removed on the
first publish, and the list says so.

A hint, validation message, or label for a group or repeat can reference a
file that workers never see or hear there. When your app has references like
these, the review explains that Nova removes them on the first publish. The
files stay in CommCare HQ.
A question or choice that shows the same file keeps showing it. If someone in CommCare HQ moves a missing picture to a different choice or adds it in another language, Nova asks you to review that change too.

If CommCare HQ no longer has the file for a menu or form icon, Nova can leave
the icon out. Web Apps may show a default icon in place of an empty or broken
picture. A fresh Android installation can stop if the release requires that
file, even when a worker can still open an earlier version. After you publish
and build a new release in CommCare HQ, Android can install it without that
file. You can also choose a replacement icon in Nova before publishing.

If a CommCare Connect app has Auto Capture Location off in CommCare HQ, the
first publish turns it on for every form. Android asks for location permission
if needed. When the worker grants it and the device gets a GPS fix, the
submitted form includes that location. If a delivery form reaches CommCare
Connect, its visit records the location. If the opportunity checks for missing
GPS, the visit passes that check. If you turn off Connect before publishing, **App settings → Request location for every form** and each form’s own switch determine the result.

When the app was available in Web Apps and had no setting for incomplete
forms, the Web Apps home screen counted it as showing incomplete forms.
Publishing with this setting off hides the Incomplete Forms tile unless another
app available to the worker shows it. To keep the tile for this app, open
**App settings**, then **Saved and incomplete forms**, and turn on **Show
incomplete forms** before publishing. The worker's saved sessions stay in
CommCare HQ.

The list follows the choice you make for where the app runs, and a change only
Android shows stays off it while the app runs in Web Apps only. Where nothing
in it changes what workers see or do, the list says so.

## Stopping

Your review belongs to you and to the app in that project space. If another member takes over the review, or the app changes in CommCare HQ, Nova shows the current choices before applying them. Where a publish would remove file references, the review lists the paths and the files CommCare HQ holds there.

An admin or owner of the Project can choose **Stop publishing here** on the
project space's card. The app in CommCare HQ stays as it is, this app stops
publishing to it, and it can be brought in again as a new app once any publish
to it that was running has finished. If a publish is still finishing when you
bring that app in, Nova says so, and you can try again in a few minutes.

## From an agent

Over MCP the same steps are `list_hq_apps`, `read_hq_app` and `bring_in_hq_app`,
and in Claude Code `/nova:bring_in`. An agent asks you for the Project, the name,
where the app runs and each table's answer; it doesn't choose them.
