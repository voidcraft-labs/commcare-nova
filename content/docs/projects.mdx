---
title: Projects & sharing
description: Personal vs shared Projects, member roles, and how to move an app between Projects.
---

A **Project** is the space your apps and their case data live in. Every account
starts with one **personal Project** — a private space that's just you — and you
can create **shared Projects** to collaborate with teammates.

## Personal Projects are private

Your personal Project is yours alone. The apps and case data in it are visible
only to you, and it **can't be shared** — there's no way to invite anyone to it.
To work with other people, you move an app into a shared Project and invite them
there.

## Shared Projects

Create a shared Project from the Project switcher at the top of the page. In a
shared Project you can invite teammates (Dimagi email addresses only), and
everyone in the Project sees the same apps and the same case data. What each
member can do is set by their **role**:

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Viewer** | Open and preview the Project's apps and case data. No edits. |
| **Editor** | Build and edit the Project's apps and case data. No member management. |
| **Admin** | Everything an Editor can do, plus invite members, change their roles, and remove them. |
| **Owner** | Whoever created the Project — everything an Admin can do, plus renaming the Project. Can't be removed. |

Deleting a Project isn't available to anyone yet, including its Owner. A Project
holds apps, case data, media, lookup tables, and conversation history, and Nova
won't offer a single control that removes all of it until that lifecycle is
fully audited. Move or delete the apps you no longer want instead.

If someone changes your role while an app is open, Nova pauses editing and
refreshes the app and your access together. Changes that were already waiting
to save stay in that browser tab. They resume saving automatically if your
current role can edit; with Viewer access, they remain paused while you can
continue exploring and previewing the app. If your access is removed entirely,
Nova closes the workspace instead of showing data from a Project you can no
longer view.

## Moving an app between Projects

On the home page, each app card has a **Move to another Project** control. Pick
a destination and choose **Move app** — Nova moves the app in one protected
operation, so it either arrives complete or stays exactly where it was.

You'll see the control when you're an **Admin** or **Owner** of the Project the
app is in, and the destination list shows only the other Projects where you hold
Admin or Owner access too. If you don't have that access on both ends, the move
isn't offered.

### What moves with the app

Everything Project-scoped travels with it:

- the app itself and its full edit history,
- its case data,
- its media (logos, images, audio), and
- its chat conversations, **including files attached in chat**.

Everyone in the destination Project can see all of it, at whatever their role
allows — so treat a move as sharing that data with those members.

### When a move waits

- **A Solutions Architect run is in progress.** An app being built or edited
  can't move; try again once the run finishes.
- **Someone still has the app open in an older browser tab.** Nova asks you to
  have them refresh first, so no one is left editing an app that has moved out
  from under them.
- **Every Owner of the current Project must already be a member of the
  destination.** This keeps an app from moving somewhere its Owners can't
  follow.

Apps that use Project data tables can't move yet — their lookup tables would be
left behind. Nova blocks those moves rather than splitting an app from its data.
