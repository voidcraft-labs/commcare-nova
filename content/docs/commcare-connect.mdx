CommCare Connect pays frontline workers for training they finish and for
visits they deliver. A Connect app in Nova is one of two kinds, chosen in
**App settings → Connect**:

- A **learn app** teaches. Each form that takes part holds a **learn module**,
  its training content, or an **assessment**, a quiz whose score Connect
  reads, or both.
- A **deliver app** records the work. Each form that takes part holds a
  **deliver unit**, a visit Connect can pay for, or a **task**, something a
  worker is assigned and finishes with that form.

A form that holds none of these stays an ordinary form of the app. You set a
form's part in its settings, or for every form at once in the Connect manager.

## Deliver units and tasks

A task goes in a form of its own. CommCare Connect checks the delivery before it
completes a task, so a form holding both a deliver unit and a task has its own
visit turned away while the task is assigned. Nova offers a form's task only
while that form holds no deliver unit, and the other way round.

## IDs

Each learn module, assessment, deliver unit and task has an ID that CommCare
Connect knows it by. Nova picks one from the name. An ID you choose yourself
starts with a letter, uses letters, digits and underscores, and is 50
characters or fewer. It also differs from every other Connect ID in the app
and from the IDs of the form's top-level fields, since the block sits beside
them in the form.

CommCare Connect keeps its records under these IDs, so it helps to settle them
before you publish. If you change a deliver unit's ID after that, Connect turns
away each delivery of the new ID until an opportunity manager adds it to a
payment unit, and CommCare HQ doesn't send those deliveries again. Connect reads
a changed learn module ID as a second module, and a task already assigned under
an old ID is never completed by the form.

## Publishing a Connect app

A Connect app's forms reach CommCare Connect only through CommCare HQ: the
project space forwards each form to Connect once a worker submits it. That
needs two things on the project space, and no API key can read either, so the
Publish dialog asks you to confirm both:

- **Data Forwarding**, a feature of the Pro, Advanced and Enterprise plans.
  Without it, CommCare HQ takes the app's forms and sends none of them to
  Connect, so no learning or delivery is recorded there.
- **A Connect forwarder.** A project space admin adds it on CommCare HQ's Data
  Forwarding page, choosing **Forward Form Metadata to Commcare Connect** with
  the connection CommCare Connect gives them.

Nova keeps your answers with that project space until you withdraw them in
**App setup → Publishing**. The CommCare HQ app file's guide names the same two
things. See [Publishing to CommCare HQ](/docs/publishing#what-the-project-spaces-plan-includes).
