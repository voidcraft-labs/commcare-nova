---
title: Manage access
description: What permissions your client gets and how to revoke them.
---

When your client signs in to Nova, you see a consent screen listing what it's asking for. Each row is one permission your client can use. You can revoke the connection from your client or from Nova at any time.

## What your client can do

| Permission | What it grants |
|---|---|
| See your name and email | Standard sign-in identity. |
| Read your apps | List, search, and summarize apps in your Nova account. |
| Create and edit apps on your behalf | Build new apps and modify existing ones. |
| See your CommCare HQ project connection | Look up which HQ project spaces your stored API key can reach. Used to preview the target before an upload. |
| Deploy your apps to CommCare HQ on your behalf | Upload finished apps to a chosen HQ project space. |
| See the members of your Projects — names and emails | List who's in your shared Projects and which invitations are pending. (Listing the Projects themselves rides the base connection; this row is about the member roster.) |
| Create Projects, invite teammates, and change member roles on your behalf | Create shared Projects, send invitations, change member roles, and move apps between Projects — acts that give other people access to your work. |

The HQ rows are separate from the rest because CommCare HQ is a different system: having Nova access doesn't automatically grant HQ access. The Projects rows are separate because they reach beyond your own account — one exposes other members' names and emails, the other hands out access. Each of these permissions stands alone (the write side doesn't include the read side), so check every row your client needs. If your client connected without one, the affected tools refuse with a clear error and you'll need to reauthorize.

## Disconnecting

The cleanest way is from your client's MCP settings, most clients have a "sign out" or "remove server" action that tells Nova to invalidate the connection.

If your client doesn't, or if you've already removed Nova from your client's config, do it from Nova: open [Settings → Connected apps](https://commcare.app/settings), find the client's entry, and remove it. Once the connection is gone, the next tool call from that client fails until you sign back in.

If your client uses an [API key](./api-keys.mdx) instead of OAuth, it doesn't appear under Connected apps, find it under **API keys** on the same settings page and click **Revoke**. The two paths are independent: revoking an OAuth grant doesn't touch your API keys, and revoking a key doesn't disturb any active OAuth sessions.

## Reauthorizing

If your client has the connection but is missing a permission you need (typically the HQ or Projects ones), tell your client to sign out and back in. The new sign-in shows the consent screen again, make sure every row you need is checked before you click Allow.

One wrinkle for clients that registered with Nova before a permission existed: such a client asked for the permission set that existed at registration time, so signing out and back in offers the same old set, and requesting a newer permission fails with `invalid_scope`. Remove the Nova server from the client and add it again — the fresh registration picks up the full current set.
